# Board brief: TextilWorld (an existing business, not a new idea)

This is a working company asking the board where to focus next, not a pre-launch idea. Score the business as it is and the strongest direction for it.

## The business
TextilWorld imports fabric from China and sells it by the meter (minimum 0.5 m; 1 Shopify unit = 0.5 m; standard width 150 cm) from a warehouse in Sękocin Nowy, near Warsaw, Poland.

Channels: own Shopify store textilworld.pl with 17 country storefronts; Allegro (Poland's largest marketplace, the main marketplace channel); Etsy; Amazon.de; Eobuwie. Emag (Romania) and Cdiscount (France) not yet active. Direct B2B sales by phone/email.

Products: viscose (wiskoza, most popular), velvet/velour (Cuba velour is a bestseller), chiffon, crepe, jersey knit, cotton (incl. medical grade), satin, muslin, fleece, Tencel, boucle, faux suede, sweatshirt fabric, quilted fabric, silk, technical fabric.

Prices: about 9.90 PLN/m (cotton) to 24.90 PLN/m (velvet).

## Known problems (live audit)
- Website: a bestselling velvet shows as "pre-order" and can't be bought; two products have no add-to-cart button; all 17 country stores show prices only in PLN; product pages only in Polish; no meta descriptions; garbled footer encoding.
- Stock: 3 of 8 tracked SKUs at zero stock, chiffon below reorder point. 50–70 day lead time from China; must order by 20 Nov 2026 before Chinese New Year.
- Couriers: repeated weight/dimension overcharges; GLS blocked; DPD small overcharges. Disputes in June: 4.20, 6.30, 42.00 and 98.00 PLN.
- Team: operations manager on leave, customer-service person on maternity leave, an intern built an internal CRM. Ads paused for summer.
- No loyalty program, no email marketing, B2B ordering is manual.

## Ideas the CEO is considering
Fix the website (currency, translations, broken products); price management across channels; a stock/reorder decision system with ABC analysis; a B2B portal with wholesale prices and NET30; expanding to Emag and Cdiscount; a 5 × 0.5 m fabric sample box at ~39.90 PLN; TikTok/YouTube fabric content; email marketing (Klaviyo/Shopify Email). Long term: own logistics center and new brands (pet products, home electronics, hair care, fitness).

## Not known
Monthly revenue, gross margin per meter, landed cost from China, shipping cost per order, rent, salaries, customer acquisition cost, repeat-purchase rate.
