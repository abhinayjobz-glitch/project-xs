# TextilWorld

- What it is: An existing Polish online fabric importer that buys fabric in China and sells it by the meter (minimum 0.5 m) through its own Shopify store (textilworld.pl, 17 country storefronts), Allegro, Etsy, Amazon.de and Eobuwie, plus direct B2B sales.
- Who it is for: B2C — small clothing brands, hobby sewers and small hobby brands, sewing schools and their students, event/decor/film-set buyers, small textile retailers. B2B — fashion schools, medical/scrubs makers (need OEKO-TEX), army/uniform suppliers, furniture upholsterers.
- What it sells, at what price: Fabric by the meter, 150 cm wide, roughly 9.90 PLN/m (plain cotton) to 24.90 PLN/m (velvet). Examples on Allegro: Wiskoza Gładka Czarna 14.90 PLN/m (competitor 16.50), Dzianina Jersey 12.50, Krepa 16.90, Szyfon 11.90 (competitor 10.50), Aksamit Cuba Welur 24.90 (competitor 22.00).
- Where and how: Warehouse in Sękocin Nowy near Warsaw. Orders flow through BaseLinker; shipped by DPD, InPost, DHL, UPS, ORLEN (GLS blocked after overcharges). Lead time from China 50–70 days.
- Budget and constraints: Already trading. Not known: monthly revenue, gross margin, fixed costs. Small team: CEO (Praveer Lakhani), operations manager on leave, customer-service person on maternity leave, an intern. Ads paused in June 2026.
