## Why they pass

| reason | buyers | in their words |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| habit | 10 | "For sets and decor I need to feel the drape and see the colour under light before I commit to 20 metres, so I go to my local fabric shop first. I'd only look at an online shop like this if my shop ran out, and nothing here tells me it's better than what I already use." (P001) · "I already have a fabric shop I trust and they know what I need, so I have no reason to gamble on a new one. 14.90 for black viscose is fine, but it isn't cheap enough to make me switch." ## What would flip a no

- A cheap swatch/sample pack I could feel first, plus live stock numbers I can trust so I know the metres are actually in the warehouse and ship next day.
- Cheap swatches or a sample pack sent to my InPost locker, so I can see and feel the real colour before ordering metres.
- Seeing their Allegro listing with Smart delivery, the real number of metres in stock, and a clear 'cut and shipped same day if ordered by X' promise.
- Cheap colour swatches sent within a couple of days, plus real stock numbers shown on the product page, so I know I can get the full length before the deadline.
- Their Allegro listing coming up as the cheapest with Smart delivery and a clear next-day dispatch, then I'd just buy it without thinking about who the seller is.
- If I could get a cheap sample swatch sent to my InPost locker first, or if my shop ran out of something I needed and they had it.
- A cheap swatch or sample box I could touch first, or seeing a sewing creator I follow actually use their velour or satin in a video.
- Cheap physical swatches or a sample card I can pick up from my InPost locker, plus a clear volume price for 10 m and up
- A cheap swatch or sample pack so I can feel the fabrics first, plus clear in-stock status with next-day InPost delivery.
- Cheap or free swatch samples posted quickly, or real daylight photos next to a colour reference, plus a bulk price per metre for 10 m and up.
- Cheap sample swatches (or a sample box) sent to my InPost locker, plus daylight photos of the actual fabric and clear stock status on each product.
- If my usual shop ran out of what I need and they had it, or a cheap swatch I could check before the wedding.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
