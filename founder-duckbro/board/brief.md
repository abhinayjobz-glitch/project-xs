# Board brief: duckbro (pre-launch D2C brand)

Everything below was read from the public website duckbro.store on 2026-10-14. Nothing else is known.

## What it is
"A serious fashion house, run by an unserious duck." Humorous duck-themed socks, bags and accessories for "humans who don't take themselves too seriously". Online only, "Est. 2026". Sold in limited "drops" ("Limited drops, unlimited vibes. When they're gone, they're gone."). The name is a joke: "duckbro: the polite way to say the other f-word in front of your nan."

## How buying works today
- Every product says "Pre-order · Not available yet" and "Drop 003 has ended · Restocking soon". The top banner still says "Drop 003 is live".
- "Pre-order the Zero-Waste Pack: Nothing ships until enough of you reserve it. Pick a product, save your spot for €4.99, credited toward your order once we hit 100 reservations and go into production." Tagline: "0 units landfilled. We only make what's reserved."
- Badges like "Almost Gone", "Restocked", "Best Seller" and "Fan Favorite" appear on items that are not in stock.
- Shipping: from an EU warehouse to the EU and UK, 2–4 business days, €4 flat, free over €50. No rest-of-world shipping yet. Stripe checkout. 30-day returns (unworn; socks must be unopened). "Duck Guarantee: If it doesn't quack, we'll fix it."
- 10% off the first order for joining the email list.
- The homepage shows three 5-star quotes (Marc, Amsterdam; Priya, Berlin; Tom, Paris). Every product page says "No reviews yet — be the first to quack."
- Socials: Instagram @duckbr0, TikTok @duck__br0, X @duckbr0. Content: a journal with 3 buying-guide articles.
