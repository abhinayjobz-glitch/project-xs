# The board on duckbro

**Vote: 2 PASS, 1 FUND IF. Average score 3.3 / 10** (Offers 3 PASS, Monopoly 3 PASS, Product 4 FUND IF).

Full memos: `board/offers.md`, `board/monopoly.md`, `board/product.md`. Everything was judged from the public site (duckbro.store, read 14 Oct 2026). Costs, reservations and followers are unknown.

## Risks all three members raised

1. **The site's trust signals look staged, and that is the most urgent fix.** All three put it first or second:
   - "Almost Gone", "Restocked" and "Best Seller" badges on items nobody can buy.
   - The banner says "Drop 003 is live" while every product says it has ended.
   - Three 5-star homepage quotes (Marc, Priya, Tom) sit beside "No reviews yet" on every product page.
   - Struck-through "was" prices on a brand that has not sold yet.

   For a brand whose only moat is trust and voice, these are self-inflicted holes. In the EU, earlier-price claims and reviews are regulated (the Omnibus Directive rules on price reductions and consumer reviews). A lawyer should confirm, but this is a real risk, not just a style point.
2. **The pre-order asks a lot and promises little.** The buyer pays €4.99 and waits for 100 strangers. There is no date, no progress counter and no stated refund if 100 is never reached.
3. **No focus and no niche.** Twelve SKUs across socks, bags, caps, scarves and charms split early demand, so each item is less likely to reach its own 100 threshold. "Humans who don't take themselves too seriously" is a mood, not a market.
4. **A copyable commodity.** Novelty socks and totes are a crowded category. The duck design and the voice are the only differences.

## Where they disagreed

- **Offers** says the market has almost no pain. To fund it, rebuild one offer around a real occasion (a gift with a delivery-date promise and a "they don't laugh, money back" guarantee) at full price, not a markdown.
- **Monopoly** wants one narrow niche duckbro can own first, proven by 100 reservations coming from that niche.
- **Product** is the most positive. The voice is genuinely good and consistent, and the one-line pitch works. Cut to one hero product and design the whole reservation experience.

## Conditions checklist

- [ ] Remove every unearned signal: stock badges on unavailable items, "was" prices never charged, and homepage quotes not from verified buyers. Fix the banner.
- [ ] Pre-order terms on the page: a deadline, an automatic refund of the €4.99 if 100 is not reached, and a public counter.
- [ ] One hero product for the next drop (the board points to a sock pack)
- [ ] Unit cost, supplier, MOQ and a production sample confirmed (`/founder-cfo`)
- [ ] 100 paid reservations as proof of demand before more spend
- [ ] Name the niche or occasion (e.g. the gift buyer before Christmas) and measure what one reservation costs to win

## The strongest version the board can see

**A gift brand, not a fashion house.** One hero product: a duck sock gift box at full price, with a clear "arrives before Christmas, or your money back" promise. Reservations get a public counter and a refund rule. Every claim on the site is true. The voice is the asset, so spend it on TikTok and Instagram rather than on fake urgency.
