## What would flip a no

- A real ship date, or seeing the socks in a TikTok that's already blowing up with people actually wearing them, so I know it's real and coming soon.
- A Trustpilot page with real reviews and photos from people who actually received their order, plus a firm delivery date.
- A guaranteed ship-by date before mid-December with the €4.99 refunded automatically if the batch doesn't run, plus actual material and factory details (organic or recycled cotton, a named EU knitter).
- A real ship date, or buying it outright with normal delivery like any other shop, so I can check out in one tap and actually get it.
- In-stock items with a guaranteed delivery date, or at least a firm 'ships by' date that's before my deadline.
- Having it in stock and shipping now, or at least a firm delivery date and a refund if it never gets made. A cheaper single pair to try would also help.
- Stock that's actually in hand and ships within a week, plus a discount code bigger than 10%, so the Quack Pack ends up near €20.
- A real ship date (or proof the 100 reservations are already reached) and some photos or reviews from people who actually got the socks and could vouch for the knit quality.
- A real in-stock option or a guaranteed delivery date within a week or two, so I could actually give it on the birthday.
- A real delivery date plus close-up photos or reviews from people who've actually got the socks. Or the deposit fully refunded if they never hit 100.
- An in-stock option or a guaranteed ship date, like 'arrives within 10 days', so I know it lands before the birthday.
- If I could just buy the Quack Pack and have it at my door within a week, I'd probably grab it on impulse, maybe as a gift.
