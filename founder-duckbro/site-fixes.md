# duckbro.store: fixes found while running the pack (read 14 Oct 2026)

Priority order. The legal points need a lawyer to confirm. They're flagged because EU consumer law regulates them.

1. **Pre-order terms.** Say on every product page what happens to the €4.99 if 100 isn't reached (automatic refund, by when). Give a target ship-by date, and show a live reservation counter ("63 / 100").
2. **Stock badges.** "Almost Gone", "Restocked", "Best Seller", "Fan Favorite" sit on items marked "Not available yet". Remove them until they're true. (EU Unfair Commercial Practices Directive: false scarcity claims.)
3. **"Was" prices.** €44.99 → €29.99 and similar on a brand with no sales history. Show one price until a lower one follows a price actually charged. (Omnibus Directive / Price Indication rules: the reference is the lowest price in the prior 30 days.)
4. **Homepage reviews.** Marc, Priya and Tom's 5-star quotes sit beside "No reviews yet — be the first to quack" on every product. Remove them, or show only verified-buyer reviews and say how reviews are checked. (Omnibus Directive requires stating whether reviews come from real buyers.)
5. **Banner contradiction.** The ticker says "Drop 003 is live" while every product says "Drop 003 has ended · Restocking soon".
6. **UK shipping wording.** The meta description says "free EU and UK shipping over 50 euro" and the FAQ says EU and UK, but the trust strip says "Orders over €50 ship free in the EU". Pick one. Also check UK VAT and customs for EU→UK parcels: "no customs charges" only holds for EU customers.
7. **"0 units landfilled."** Sustainability-minded buyers read it as an unproven slogan. Back it with specifics (who knits, where, what happens to offcuts and unclaimed items) or soften it. (EU green-claims rules are tightening.)
8. **Focus.** 12 SKUs each need 100 reservations. Lead the next drop with one hero item (the Quack Pack or a gift box) so the first threshold is reachable.
